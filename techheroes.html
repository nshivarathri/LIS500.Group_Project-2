Our Tech Hero

One hidden tech hero that we researched and admired is Margaret Hamilton. She was a computer scientist and software engineer who worked on the Apollo space program and helped develop the software for the Apollo Guidance Computer. Her work was extremely important because the software had to be reliable enough to support astronauts during missions to the moon. Hamilton also helped push software engineering to be taken more seriously as a field. What we find most inspiring about her is that she made such a major ontribution to technology at a time when women were very overlooked in the worlds of computing and engineering. Her work had a huge impact on space exploration and she continues to be a role model for all people interested in computer science and software engineering today.
