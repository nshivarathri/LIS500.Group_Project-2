Meet Our Team

We are students in LIS 500 - Code and Power with different academic backgrounds and interests. Through this project, we are exploring how technology connects with people, power, representation, and bias. Learn more about each of us and what brought us to this course.

POSTAL CREW / 01

Yuthika Goel

Hello! My name is Yuthika Goel, and I am a junior studying Computer Science and Data Science with a certificate in Digital Studies. I decided to take this class because I wanted to explore the intersection between technology, its users, and its creators.

As a woman interested in technology and motorsport, specifically Formula 1, I am interested in understanding why women remain underrepresented in both fields and how bias and inequality can contribute to these disparities. I hope to use what I learn in this course to think more critically about the technology I create and the industries I hope to work in.

POSTAL CREW / 02

Nireekshana Shivarathri

Hi! I’m Nireekshana. I’m a junior studying Information Science, originally from Telangana, India. I’m taking this course both for a requirement and because I love where technology and people overlap.

I’m hoping to become a business analyst after I graduate, so I’m interested in learning how technology affects different communities and how we can build systems that work for everyone. I also hope to gain more hands-on experience with web scripting through this project.

POSTAL CREW / 03

Madeline Root

Hi I’m Maddie Root. I’m an information science and communication arts major. I am from Baltimore, Maryland and a senior at UW-Madison. I want to work in tech and business when I graduate. I am taking this class as a requirement for my major!

My academic interests have made me interested in the relationship between information, communication, and the ways people interact with technology.
